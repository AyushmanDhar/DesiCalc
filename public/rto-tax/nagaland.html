Vehicle Registration Process in Nagaland

Nagaland, the "Land of Festivals," operates its vehicle registration system through the Nagaland Transport Department under the administrative control of the Commissioner of Transport. The state has RTO offices in Dimapur (NL 07), Kohima (NL 01), Mokokchung (NL 02), Tuensang (NL 03), Mon (NL 09), Zunheboto (NL 10), Wokha (NL 06), Phek (NL 04), Kiphire (NL 11), Longleng (NL 12), Noklak (NL 13), and Peren (NL 08). Dimapur, being the commercial hub and entry point to Nagaland, handles the highest number of vehicle registrations in the state.

Nagaland's unique position as a state requiring Inner Line Permit (ILP) for entry affects vehicle movement and registration policies. Vehicles registered in Nagaland are permitted to ply within the state and to neighbouring states under certain conditions. The road network in Nagaland ranges from well-maintained highways in Dimapur to rugged mountain roads in districts like Mon and Kiphire, making SUVs and all-terrain vehicles a common sight. The vehicle registration process has been streamlined with the introduction of the Vahan online platform, allowing applicants to track their application status digitally.

Required Documents for Registration

  • Form 20 (application for registration of a motor vehicle)
  • Form 21 (certificate of sale by the dealer)
  • Form 22 (road worthiness certificate)
  • Valid insurance certificate
  • Address proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport, or utility bill)
  • Identity proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Driving Licence)
  • Original invoice and delivery challan
  • PUC certificate
  • Passport-size photographs (2 copies)

How Road Tax Is Calculated in Nagaland

Nagaland applies a flat percentage-based road tax of 5% of the ex-showroom price on all vehicle categories — petrol, diesel, CNG and electric cars as well as two-wheelers. The flat structure means there is no slab system or fuel-based price difference: the tax is simply 5% of the vehicle's cost, making it easy for buyers to predict their RTO outlay.

For a mid-range petrol sedan with an ex-showroom price of ₹12,00,000, the road tax calculation is: ₹12,00,000 × 5% = ₹60,000. Adding the registration fee of ₹600 and HSRP charges of ₹400, the total RTO cost equals ₹61,000. A diesel SUV of the same price now pays the same ₹60,000 — the earlier higher diesel rate has been unified at 5%.

Electric Vehicle Tax in Nagaland

Nagaland no longer exempts electric vehicles from road tax. The earlier EV exemption has been removed — electric vehicles now pay the flat 5% life tax like every other vehicle, plus the standard ₹600 registration fee and ₹400 HSRP. An EV priced at ₹12,00,000 pays ₹60,000 in road tax, exactly like a petrol or diesel car of the same price.

Two-Wheeler Tax Rates

Two-wheelers in Nagaland pay the same flat 5% of ex-showroom price. The registration fee is ₹300 and HSRP ₹200.

Commercial Vehicle Tax in Nagaland

Commercial vehicles in Nagaland pay tax based on seating capacity — small vehicles pay a one-time amount, while larger vehicles pay per seat per year:

Seats Tax
1–6 seats₹3,500 (one-time)
7–12 seats₹1,200 per seat per year
13 seats and above₹2,000 per seat per year
