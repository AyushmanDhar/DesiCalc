Vehicle Registration Process in Odisha — Step by Step

Vehicle registration in Odisha is administered by the Odisha Transport Department through Regional Transport Offices (RTOs) in every district, running on the national VAHAN platform. Non-transport vehicles (private cars and two-wheelers) pay a one-time motor vehicle tax at registration — there is no annual road tax for private vehicles in Odisha. Tax for a new vehicle is payable at the time of registration, and permanent registration must be completed within the validity of the temporary registration.

  1. Buy the vehicle and get a temporary registration. The dealer issues a temporary registration (application in Form 20) at the time of purchase. A temporary registration is valid for a maximum of one month (Rule 47, Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989), within which the permanent registration must be completed.
  2. Submit the registration application. Form 20 (application for registration), Form 21 (sale certificate from the dealer/manufacturer), Form 22 (certificate of roadworthiness), valid insurance, and proof of address and identity are filed — normally by the dealer for new vehicles.
  3. Pay road tax and registration fee. Road tax is a one-time payment calculated on the ex-showroom price. The registration fee under Rule 81 of CMVR 1989 is ₹300 for a motorcycle and ₹600 for a non-transport light motor vehicle. HSRP is ₹400 for four-wheelers and ₹200 for two-wheelers.
  4. Registration number is allotted. On verification of documents and tax payment, VAHAN allots a permanent number in the OD-XX-XXXX format and the RC is issued to the owner.

Required Documents for Registration

  • Form 20 (duly filled application form)
  • Form 21 (Sale Certificate from authorised dealer)
  • Form 22 (Certificate of Roadworthiness)
  • Insurance Certificate (Third-party or Comprehensive)
  • Address Proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport, Utility Bill)
  • Identity Proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Driving Licence)
  • Original Invoice and Delivery Challan
  • PUC Certificate
  • Passport-size photographs (2 copies)

How Odisha Road Tax is Calculated

Formula: Ex-showroom price of the vehicle × applicable slab rate % = one-time road tax payable. Odisha uses the same slab structure for petrol, diesel and CNG vehicles — the fuel does not change the rate. The slab rate applies to the full ex-showroom price of the vehicle.

Example: A car priced at ₹10 lakh (ex-showroom) falls in the ₹5–10 lakh slab → 8% → Road tax = ₹80,000. Add ₹600 registration fee and ₹400 HSRP, and total RTO charges come to ₹81,000.

The official slab structure for non-transport vehicles in Odisha (one-time tax):

  • Cars up to ₹5 lakh: 6%
  • Cars ₹5–10 lakh: 8%
  • Cars ₹10–20 lakh: 10%
  • Cars ₹20–40 lakh: 12%
  • Cars above ₹40 lakh: 20%
  • Two-wheelers: the same 6/8/10/12/20% slabs by price

EV Policy: What Odisha EV Buyers Save

Electric vehicles are fully exempt from road tax in Odisha — across two-wheelers, three-wheelers and four-wheelers, with no price cap. As part of the state's commitment to electric mobility, the registration fee is also waived for EVs, so the only RTO charge an EV buyer pays is the HSRP fee (₹400 for a four-wheeler, ₹200 for a two-wheeler).

Example: an electric car priced at ₹15 lakh pays ₹0 life tax and ₹0 registration fee — just ₹400 for HSRP. The same car in petrol would pay ₹1,50,000 road tax (10% slab) plus ₹600 registration and ₹400 HSRP. This makes Odisha one of the most attractive states in eastern India for EV buyers.

Note: The exemption applies to vehicles purchased and registered in Odisha. Always confirm the current notification on the official Odisha Transport Department portal before you finalise a purchase.

Where to Pay Odisha Road Tax Online

You can pay motor vehicle tax online on either of the two official portals:

  1. VAHAN (vahan.parivahan.gov.in): Enter the vehicle registration number → Online Services → Pay Vehicle Tax → enter the last 5 digits of the chassis number and validate → generate OTP (sent to the registered mobile number) → update insurance details → review the fee panel → pay. A payment receipt is generated immediately.
  2. Odisha e-Transport portal: The state's e-Transport portal allows you to pay road tax and registration fees online using debit cards, credit cards, or net banking.
