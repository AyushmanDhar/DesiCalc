Tamil Nadu road tax by vehicle type

Tamil Nadu charges a one-time motor vehicle tax on private non-transport vehicles at registration. The rate rises with the ex-showroom price for cars, while two-wheelers use a separate two-band structure and electric vehicles are fully exempt.

Cars and SUVs

Private cars and SUVs pay 12% up to ₹5 lakh, 13% from ₹5,00,001 to ₹10 lakh, 18% from ₹10,00,001 to ₹20 lakh and 20% above ₹20 lakh. The slab rate applies to the full ex-showroom price. A ₹8 lakh car pays ₹8,00,000 × 13% = ₹1,04,000; with ₹600 registration and ₹400 HSRP the total is ₹1,05,000. A ₹15 lakh car moves to the 18% slab and pays ₹2,70,000 in tax.

Two-wheelers

Motorcycles and scooters pay 10% up to ₹1 lakh and 12% above ₹1 lakh. A ₹1,50,000 bike pays ₹1,50,000 × 12% = ₹18,000; registration is ₹300 and HSRP is ₹200, giving ₹18,500 at registration. A ₹90,000 bike stays in the 10% band and pays ₹9,000.

Electric vehicles

Electric vehicles are fully exempt from one-time road tax in Tamil Nadu across all categories and price points. The exemption was granted under the Tamil Nadu Electric Vehicles Policy 2023 by G.O. (Ms) No. 17 dated 13 January 2023, and extended by G.O. (Ms) No. 674 dated 29 December 2025 until 31 December 2027. There is no price cap. The registration fee and HSRP are not waived: an electric car pays ₹600 registration plus ₹400 HSRP, or ₹1,000 in total. This makes the saving largest on premium cars — a ₹25 lakh electric car avoids the 20% slab, which would be ₹5,00,000 on a petrol car of the same price.

Commercial vehicles

Commercial vehicles with 1–4 seats pay a one-time ₹3,000. Vehicles with 5–7 seats and 8–13 seats pay ₹200 per seat per quarter; 14–35 seats pay ₹400 per seat per quarter, and 36 seats or more pay ₹500 per seat per quarter. A 10-seat commercial vehicle therefore pays ₹2,000 per quarter, or ₹8,000 a year, before registration and HSRP.

Vehicle Registration Process in Tamil Nadu — Step by Step

Where to register

Vehicle registration in Tamil Nadu is administered by the Tamil Nadu Transport Department through a network of over 40 RTO offices across all districts, running on the national VAHAN platform. Major hubs include Chennai (TN 01 to TN 10, TN 20, TN 21), Coimbatore (TN 37 to TN 41), Madurai (TN 58 to TN 60), Trichy (TN 45, TN 48), Salem (TN 27, TN 30) and Tirunelveli (TN 72, TN 76). Non-transport vehicles (private cars and two-wheelers) pay a one-time motor vehicle tax at registration — there is no annual road tax for private vehicles in Tamil Nadu. Tax for a new vehicle is payable at the time of registration.

  1. Buy the vehicle and get a temporary registration. The dealer issues a temporary registration (application in Form 20) at the time of purchase. A temporary registration is valid for a maximum of one month (Rule 47, Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989), within which the permanent registration must be completed.
  2. Submit the registration application. Form 20 (application for registration), Form 21 (sale certificate from the dealer/manufacturer), Form 22 (certificate of roadworthiness), valid insurance, and proof of address and identity are filed — normally by the dealer for new vehicles. Add Form 34 (hypothecation agreement) for loan-financed vehicles.
  3. Pay road tax and registration fee. Road tax is a one-time payment calculated on the ex-showroom price. The registration fee under Rule 81 of CMVR 1989 is ₹300 for a motorcycle and ₹600 for a non-transport light motor vehicle. HSRP is ₹400 for four-wheelers and ₹200 for two-wheelers.
  4. Registration number is allotted. On verification of documents and tax payment, VAHAN allots a permanent number in the TN-XX-XXXX format and the RC is issued to the owner.

Required Documents for Registration

  • Form 20 (duly filled application form)
  • Form 21 (Sale Certificate from authorised dealer)
  • Form 22 (Certificate of Roadworthiness)
  • Insurance Certificate (Third-party or Comprehensive)
  • Address Proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport, Utility Bill)
  • Identity Proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Driving Licence)
  • Original Invoice and Delivery Challan
  • PUC Certificate
  • Form 34 (hypothecation agreement for loan vehicles)
  • Passport-size photographs (2 copies)

Fees and timelines

The registration fee is ₹600 for a non-transport light motor vehicle and ₹300 for a motorcycle. HSRP costs ₹400 for a four-wheeler and ₹200 for a two-wheeler. The dealer issues a temporary registration valid for one month, and the permanent RC is issued after the RTO verifies the vehicle and documents. Dealer-assisted registration at a busy Chennai or Coimbatore RTO typically takes one to two weeks; a direct application can take longer depending on the appointment queue.

How Road Tax Is Calculated in Tamil Nadu

Tamil Nadu uses a progressive slab-based road tax system for private vehicles, where the tax rate increases with the vehicle's ex-showroom price. The slab rate applies to the full ex-showroom price of the vehicle. Vehicles priced up to ₹5,00,000 are taxed at 12%. From ₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000, the rate rises to 13%. Vehicles between ₹10,00,001 and ₹20,00,000 attract 18% tax, and vehicles above ₹20,00,000 are taxed at the highest rate of 20%.

For example, a compact car priced at ₹6,00,000 falls in the 13% slab: ₹6,00,000 × 13% = ₹78,000 road tax, plus ₹600 registration and ₹400 HSRP, totalling ₹79,000. A mid-size sedan at ₹10,00,000 also falls in the 13% slab: ₹10,00,000 × 13% = ₹1,30,000 road tax, total RTO cost ₹1,31,000. A luxury car at ₹25,00,000 in the 20% slab would incur ₹25,00,000 × 20% = ₹5,00,000 in road tax alone, totalling ₹5,01,000. An electric car at any price point would pay just ₹1,000 (registration + HSRP), making the slab system highly punitive for ICE vehicles at higher price points but very rewarding for EV buyers.

For two-wheelers, the structure is separate: 10% up to ₹1 lakh and 12% above ₹1 lakh.

EV Policy: What Tamil Nadu EV Buyers Save

Tamil Nadu offers a full road tax exemption for electric vehicles across all categories and price points. The exemption is particularly significant in Tamil Nadu given the higher slab rates for ICE vehicles. The state's comprehensive EV policy includes capital subsidies for EV manufacturing, charging infrastructure development, and demand-side incentives for early EV adopters.

Example: an electric car priced at ₹25 lakh pays ₹0 road tax — compared with ₹5,00,000 (20% slab) for a petrol car at the same price. The only RTO charges an EV buyer pays are the registration fee (₹600) and HSRP (₹400), a total of ₹1,000.

Chennai, Coimbatore and Madurai have seen growing EV charging networks, supported by both government and private investments. Note: The exemption applies to vehicles purchased and registered in Tamil Nadu. Always confirm the current notification with the Tamil Nadu Transport Department before you finalise a purchase.

Where to Pay Tamil Nadu Road Tax Online

You can pay motor vehicle tax online on the official portals:

  1. VAHAN (vahan.parivahan.gov.in): Enter the vehicle registration number → Online Services → Pay Vehicle Tax → enter the last 5 digits of the chassis number and validate → generate OTP (sent to the registered mobile number) → update insurance details → review the fee panel → pay. A payment receipt is generated immediately.
  2. Tamil Nadu Transport Department: The department's portal (tn.gov.in/transport) provides online services for tax payment, HSRP booking and application tracking.

Frequently asked questions

Is road tax in Tamil Nadu one-time or annual?

Private cars and two-wheelers pay a one-time lifetime motor vehicle tax at registration. There is no annual road tax for private non-transport vehicles. Commercial vehicles pay quarterly per seat, except the 1–4 seat bracket, which pays a one-time ₹3,000.

How much is road tax for a ₹10 lakh car in Tamil Nadu?

A car with an ex-showroom price of ₹10 lakh falls in the ₹5–10 lakh slab and pays 13% one-time road tax, i.e. ₹1,30,000. The registration fee is ₹600 and HSRP is ₹400, so total RTO charges come to ₹1,31,000.

What is the road tax for two-wheelers in Tamil Nadu?

Two-wheelers pay one-time road tax of 10% for models up to ₹1 lakh and 12% above ₹1 lakh. The registration fee for a motorcycle is ₹300 and HSRP is ₹200.

Are electric vehicles exempt from road tax in Tamil Nadu?

Yes. Electric vehicles registered in Tamil Nadu are fully exempt from one-time road tax across all categories and price points. The exemption was granted by G.O. (Ms) No. 17 dated 13 January 2023 and extended by G.O. (Ms) No. 674 dated 29 December 2025 until 31 December 2027. There is no price cap. The registration fee (₹600 for a car) and HSRP charge (₹400) still apply.

How much does HSRP cost in Tamil Nadu?

HSRP costs ₹400 for a four-wheeler and ₹200 for a two-wheeler. New vehicles get the plate fitted by the dealer; the charge is shown separately from road tax and registration.

Can I get a road tax refund if I sell or transfer my vehicle?

Road tax is generally not refunded on a sale inside Tamil Nadu; the lifetime tax stays with the vehicle. If the vehicle is re-registered in another state, you can apply for a refund of the unexpired portion with the NOC and re-registration proof. Refunds for total loss or export are considered case by case.

How do I register an out-of-state vehicle in Tamil Nadu?

Obtain the NOC from the original RTO, then apply for re-registration at the Tamil Nadu RTO covering your address. The vehicle is inspected, road tax is charged on the depreciated value at Tamil Nadu rates, and a TN registration mark is assigned.

What is the penalty for late registration or late tax payment in Tamil Nadu?

A new vehicle must be registered within 30 days of delivery. After that the RTO levies a late registration fee, and unpaid road tax attracts penalty and interest under the Tamil Nadu Motor Vehicles Taxation Act. The exact amount depends on the delay and is calculated by the RTO.

Can I register my vehicle in Tamil Nadu with an Aadhaar address from another state?

No, you must provide valid Tamil Nadu address proof to register a vehicle in the state. Aadhaar with a TN address works, but an out-of-state Aadhaar needs additional proof of residence.

What is the green tax in Tamil Nadu?

Tamil Nadu imposes a green tax on older vehicles — over 15 years for private vehicles and 10 years for commercial vehicles — as a disincentive for running polluting vehicles. Electric vehicles are exempt from PUC requirements because they have no tailpipe emissions.
