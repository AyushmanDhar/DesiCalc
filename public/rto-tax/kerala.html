Kerala road tax by vehicle type

Kerala levies a one-time lifetime tax on private non-transport vehicles under the Kerala Motor Vehicles Taxation Act, 1976. Cars use a five-band price schedule, two-wheelers use three bands, and electric vehicles pay concessional slabs rather than the full ICE rate.

Cars and SUVs

Private cars pay 10% up to ₹5 lakh, 13% from ₹5–10 lakh, 15% from ₹10–15 lakh, 17% from ₹15–20 lakh and 22% above ₹20 lakh. The rate applies to the full ex-showroom price, and petrol, diesel and CNG follow the same slabs. A ₹8 lakh car pays ₹8,00,000 × 13% = ₹1,04,000; with ₹600 registration and ₹1,000 HSRP the total is ₹1,05,600. A ₹15 lakh car pays ₹2,25,000 in tax.

Two-wheelers

Motorcycles and scooters pay 13% up to ₹1 lakh, 15% from ₹1–2 lakh and 21% above ₹2 lakh. A ₹1,50,000 bike pays ₹1,50,000 × 15% = ₹22,500; registration is ₹300 and HSRP is ₹500, giving ₹23,300 at registration. A ₹2,50,000 bike moves to the 21% band and pays ₹52,500.

Electric vehicles

Kerala does not fully exempt electric cars. After the 19 June 2026 state budget revision, EVs pay 3% up to ₹10 lakh, 5% from ₹10–20 lakh, 10% from ₹20–40 lakh and 15% above ₹40 lakh. Electric two-wheelers and three-wheelers pay 5%. A ₹15 lakh electric car therefore pays ₹75,000 tax, plus ₹600 registration and ₹1,000 HSRP, or ₹76,600 in total. The earlier 1 April 2025 schedule had taxed EVs up to ₹15 lakh at 5%, ₹15–20 lakh at 8% and above ₹20 lakh at 10%; the June 2026 revision replaced those bands.

Commercial vehicles

Commercial vehicles with 1–3 seats pay a one-time ₹3,000. Larger vehicles pay per seat per quarter: ₹300 for 4–5 seats, ₹400 for 6–12 seats, ₹500 for 13–20 seats and ₹600 for 21 seats or more. A 10-seat commercial vehicle pays ₹400 × 10 = ₹4,000 per quarter, or ₹16,000 a year, before registration and HSRP.

Vehicle Registration Process in Kerala

Where to register

Vehicle registration in Kerala is handled by the Motor Vehicles Department (MVD), which runs 18 Regional Transport Offices (RTOs) and about 68 sub-RTOs across the state's 14 districts. The Transport Commissionerate is headquartered at Trans Towers, Vazhuthacaud, Thiruvananthapuram. The official MVD directory at mvd.kerala.gov.in lists every office with its address, phone number and email contact.

Registration follows the national VAHAN platform. When you buy a new vehicle, the dealer usually handles the paperwork: the vehicle first gets a temporary registration so it can be driven while the permanent registration is processed, and permanent registration must be completed within a month of delivery. The RTO physically inspects the vehicle, verifies the chassis and engine numbers, and on approval issues a registration mark in the KL-XX-XXXX format. The road tax is collected at this point as a one-time lifetime tax valid for 15 years. New vehicles come with the HSRP (high security registration plate) already fitted by the manufacturer, and the printed registration certificate is delivered to your address.

Required Documents for Registration

  • Form 20 (Application for Registration)
  • Form 21 (Dealer's Sale Certificate)
  • Form 22 (Manufacturer's Roadworthiness Certificate)
  • Valid insurance policy (third-party or comprehensive)
  • Address proof (Aadhaar, voter ID, passport, utility bill)
  • Identity proof (Aadhaar, PAN, driving licence)
  • Original vehicle invoice and delivery note
  • PUC (Pollution Under Control) certificate
  • HSRP booking receipt
  • Passport-size photographs

Fees and timelines

The registration fee is ₹600 for a non-transport light motor vehicle and ₹300 for a motorcycle. HSRP costs about ₹1,000–₹1,200 for a four-wheeler and ₹500–₹600 for a two-wheeler. Permanent registration must be completed within one month of delivery; the dealer usually handles the paperwork, and the RC is posted after the RTO inspection and tax payment. Expect roughly one to two weeks for dealer-assisted registration and longer for a direct application at a busy district RTO.

Road Tax Calculation in Kerala

Kerala levies road tax under the Kerala Motor Vehicles Taxation Act, 1976, as a one-time lifetime tax on private non-transport vehicles. It is charged as a percentage of the vehicle's purchase (ex-showroom) value, with progressive slabs — the more expensive the vehicle, the higher the rate. Unlike some states, Kerala does not charge a separate higher rate for diesel cars: petrol, diesel, CNG and hybrid vehicles all follow the same price slabs.

The current slabs for private cars, per the MVD tax schedule updated on 1 April 2025, are: 10% up to ₹5 lakh, 13% from ₹5-10 lakh, 15% from ₹10-15 lakh, 17% from ₹15-20 lakh, and 22% above ₹20 lakh. The progression is steep: a ₹8 lakh car attracts ₹1,04,000 in tax, a ₹10 lakh car ₹1,30,000, a ₹12 lakh car ₹1,80,000, and a ₹15 lakh car ₹2,25,000. A luxury car priced at ₹30 lakh costs ₹6,60,000 in road tax alone, before registration, HSRP and insurance. These are among the highest rates in India, and the slabs were raised in April 2023 and again in the April 2025 revision.

The one-time tax covers the full 15-year registration period. When a private vehicle crosses 15 years, its registration is renewed for 5 years at a time, and the April 2025 revision increased renewal charges: motorcycles and private three-wheelers pay ₹400 more per renewal, while cars pay ₹3,200 more (up to 750 kg), ₹4,300 more (750-1,500 kg) or ₹5,300 more (above 1,500 kg).

EV Road Tax in Kerala

Kerala does not fully exempt electric cars from road tax — it applies concessional slab rates instead. Under the revisions effective 1 April 2025, EVs up to ₹15 lakh were taxed at 5%, ₹15-20 lakh at 8% and above ₹20 lakh at 10% (previously all EVs paid a flat 5%). The revised state budget presented on 19 June 2026 restructured the bands again, and the current rates are:

  • EVs up to ₹10 lakh: 3% (down from 5%)
  • EVs ₹10-20 lakh: 5% (the ₹10-15 lakh band was unchanged; ₹15-20 lakh was cut from 8%)
  • EVs ₹20-40 lakh: 10%
  • EVs above ₹40 lakh: 15% (up from 10%)

The cut for the ₹15-20 lakh band is the headline change — on a ₹20 lakh electric car the saving at registration is up to ₹60,000. The rise for luxury EVs above ₹40 lakh is just as deliberate: a ₹50 lakh EV now costs ₹2,50,000 more in tax than before. Electric two-wheelers and three-wheelers continue to be taxed at 5%. There is no confirmed state cash subsidy for private EVs in Kerala, though EVs still enjoy the lower central GST rate of 5% (against 28% for most petrol and diesel cars).

Two-Wheeler Tax in Kerala

Two-wheelers are also taxed on a purchase-value slab basis. Under the MVD schedule updated on 1 April 2025, motorcycles and scooters up to ₹1 lakh pay 13% of the purchase value and those between ₹1-2 lakh pay 15%, with higher brackets above ₹2 lakh. The registration fee is ₹300 and the HSRP costs roughly ₹500-600. Electric two-wheelers pay the concessional 5% rate.

Paying Kerala Road Tax Online

Road tax payments in Kerala can be made online through the official MVD Kerala Service Portal (the e-tax service) or through the national Parivahan/Vahan portal. You need your vehicle's registration number and the last digits of the chassis number; the portal shows the amount due, and payment can be made by net banking, UPI, debit or credit card. A digital receipt is generated immediately — keep it as proof of compliance for traffic checks and registration renewals.

For new vehicles, the dealer normally collects the road tax and registration fees and remits them to the RTO before delivery, which is why the tax is embedded in the on-road price you negotiate. If you register a vehicle yourself, the tax must be settled before the registration certificate is issued.

HSRP (High Security Registration Plate) in Kerala

HSRP plates are mandatory for all vehicles in Kerala. Vehicles registered after 1 April 2019 already have them fitted by the manufacturer. For vehicles registered before that date, the Kerala High Court (orders dated 29 November 2024 and 27 August 2025) directed that HSRPs be procured and fitted through the vehicle manufacturer's authorised dealers. Owners book online through the official portal (bookmyhsrp.com / makemyhsrp.com), enter the registration, chassis and engine numbers, pay the fee, and book an appointment at an authorised fitment centre.

HSRP costs about ₹1,000-1,200 for a four-wheeler and ₹500-600 for a two-wheeler (excluding GST). The colour-coded fuel sticker (light blue for petrol/CNG, orange for diesel, green for EVs) is fitted with the plate. Driving without an HSRP attracts a ₹5,000 fine for the first offence and ₹10,000 for repeat offences.

RTO Offices and Registration Codes in Kerala

Kerala number plates carry the KL prefix followed by the RTO code. The 14 district offices are KL-01 Thiruvananthapuram, KL-02 Kollam, KL-03 Pathanamthitta, KL-04 Alappuzha, KL-05 Kottayam, KL-06 Idukki, KL-07 Ernakulam, KL-08 Thrissur, KL-09 Palakkad, KL-10 Malappuram, KL-11 Kozhikode, KL-12 Wayanad, KL-13 Kannur and KL-14 Kasaragod. KL-15 is reserved for the Nationalised Sector (State Transport), and additional RTOs have been added at Attingal (KL-16), Muvattupuzha (KL-17) and Vadakkara (KL-18). Sub-RTO offices continue from KL-19 (Parassala) onwards — more than 80 offices in all. The full list, with phone numbers and email addresses, is on the MVD directory page.

Vehicles Brought into Kerala from Other States

If you move to Kerala with a vehicle registered in another state, you can use it for a limited period, after which it must be re-registered in Kerala. The process starts with obtaining a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the original RTO through the Parivahan portal. The NOC and vehicle documents are then submitted to the Kerala RTO, where the road tax is calculated on the vehicle's depreciated value — the MVD's schedule assesses a vehicle under one year old at 100% of its purchase value, falling to 67% at 5-6 years and about 7% by 14-15 years. Once the Kerala tax is paid, the vehicle is re-registered with a KL mark, and you can claim a refund of the unexpired portion of the lifetime tax from the original state.

Frequently asked questions

Is road tax in Kerala one-time or annual?

Private cars and two-wheelers pay a one-time lifetime tax at registration, valid for the 15-year registration period. There is no annual road tax for private non-transport vehicles. Commercial vehicles pay per seat every quarter, except the 1–3 seat bracket, which pays a one-time ₹3,000.

How much is road tax for a ₹10 lakh car in Kerala?

A ₹10 lakh car falls in the ₹5–10 lakh slab and pays 13% one-time tax — ₹1,30,000. The same rate applies to petrol, diesel and CNG vehicles. Registration is ₹600 and HSRP is about ₹1,000–₹1,200, so the RTO total is about ₹1,31,600.

What is the road tax for two-wheelers in Kerala?

Two-wheelers pay 13% up to ₹1 lakh, 15% from ₹1–2 lakh and 21% above ₹2 lakh. A ₹1,50,000 bike pays ₹22,500 tax, plus ₹300 registration and ₹500 HSRP, or ₹23,300.

Are electric vehicles exempt from road tax in Kerala?

No. Kerala charges EVs reduced slab rates instead of full exemption: 3% up to ₹10 lakh, 5% between ₹10–20 lakh, 10% between ₹20–40 lakh and 15% above ₹40 lakh, after the 19 June 2026 budget revision. Electric two-wheelers and three-wheelers are taxed at 5%.

How much does HSRP cost in Kerala?

HSRP costs about ₹1,000–₹1,200 for a four-wheeler and ₹500–₹600 for a two-wheeler, excluding GST. Vehicles registered after 1 April 2019 already have the plate from the factory; older vehicles must book through the official portal and get it fitted at an authorised centre.

Can I get a road tax refund if I sell or transfer my vehicle?

Lifetime tax is not refunded on a sale inside Kerala; it stays with the vehicle. If you move the vehicle to another state and re-register it there, you can claim a refund of the unexpired portion of the Kerala lifetime tax from the original RTO with the NOC and re-registration proof.

How do I register an out-of-state vehicle in Kerala?

Obtain the NOC from the original RTO through the Parivahan portal, then submit the NOC and vehicle documents to the Kerala RTO. Road tax is calculated on the depreciated value — 100% of purchase value under one year, falling to about 67% at 5–6 years and about 7% at 14–15 years. The vehicle is then re-registered with a KL mark.

What is the penalty for late registration or late tax payment in Kerala?

A new vehicle must be registered within one month of delivery. After that the RTO levies a late registration fee, and unpaid road tax attracts penalty and interest under the Kerala Motor Vehicles Taxation Act, 1976. The exact amount depends on the delay and is calculated by the MVD.

How do I pay road tax online in Kerala?

Use the MVD Kerala Service Portal (services.mvd.kerala.gov.in) e-tax service or the national Parivahan/Vahan portal. Enter your registration number and chassis details, confirm the amount, pay by net banking, UPI or card, and download the receipt. For new vehicles, the dealer usually collects and remits the tax before delivery.

Does Kerala have the highest road tax in India?

Kerala is among the highest — its top slab of 22% above ₹20 lakh is one of India's steepest. Effective rates are similar in Karnataka (13–18% plus a cess) and Telangana (13–21%). For luxury EVs above ₹40 lakh, the 15% rate is now among the highest EV rates in the country.
