Andhra Pradesh road tax by vehicle type

Andhra Pradesh charges a one-time life tax on private non-transport vehicles, based on the ex-showroom price, plus a 10% Safety Cess on the tax amount. The same car slabs apply to petrol, diesel and CNG; two-wheelers have a separate flat rate. Commercial vehicles are taxed by seating capacity and pay annually or at a one-time rate depending on the bracket.

Cars and SUVs

Private cars and SUVs pay 12% up to ₹10 lakh and 14% above ₹10 lakh, with the 10% Safety Cess added to the tax. A ₹8 lakh car therefore pays ₹8,00,000 × 12% = ₹96,000 tax plus ₹9,600 cess, ₹1,05,600 in all; adding the ₹600 registration fee and ₹590 HSRP gives ₹1,06,790 at registration. A ₹15 lakh car moves to the 14% slab and pays ₹2,10,000 tax plus ₹21,000 cess.

Two-wheelers

Motorcycles and scooters pay a flat 9% of ex-showroom price, plus the 10% Safety Cess on the tax. A ₹1,00,000 bike pays ₹9,000 tax plus ₹900 cess, or ₹9,900; with the ₹300 registration fee and ₹320 HSRP, the RTO total is ₹10,520. Because the rate is flat, a ₹1,50,000 bike pays ₹14,850 including cess, not a higher percentage.

Electric vehicles

Electric vehicles are fully exempt from road tax in Andhra Pradesh under the Andhra Pradesh Sustainable Electric Mobility Policy 4.0 (2024–29), issued by G.O.Ms.No. 88 dated 11 December 2024. The policy provides 100% road tax exemption for five years and covers battery electric cars, two-wheelers and three-wheelers across all price points; no price cap is stated in the policy. The registration fee is also waived for electric cars and electric two-wheelers, so an EV pays only the HSRP charge at first registration — ₹590 for a car and ₹320 for a two-wheeler.

Commercial vehicles

Commercial vehicles pay by seating capacity. Vehicles with 1–6 seats pay a one-time ₹5,000. Vehicles with 7–12 seats pay ₹2,000 per seat per year, 13–30 seats pay ₹3,000 per seat per year, and 31 seats or more pay ₹4,000 per seat per year. A 10-seat commercial vehicle therefore pays ₹20,000 a year, while a 20-seat bus pays ₹60,000 a year before registration and HSRP.

Vehicle Registration Process in Andhra Pradesh

Where to register

Registering a vehicle in Andhra Pradesh involves several steps handled by the State Transport Department (AP RTO). Whether you purchase a new car, bike, or commercial vehicle, you must complete registration within 30 days of purchase to avoid penalties.

The process begins with the dealer submitting Form 20 on your behalf for temporary registration. You then visit the nearest RTO office with the required documents for permanent registration. The RTO verifies the vehicle, assigns a registration number (AP-XX-XXXX format), and issues the Registration Certificate (RC).

Required Documents for Registration

  • Form 20 (Application for Registration)
  • Form 21 (Certificate of Sale from Dealer)
  • Form 22 (Roadworthiness Certificate)
  • Valid Insurance Policy (Third-party or Comprehensive)
  • Address Proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport, Utility Bill)
  • Age Proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Birth Certificate)
  • Invoice and Sales Receipt from Dealer
  • PUC (Pollution Under Control) Certificate
  • HSRP number plate order confirmation

Fees and timelines

The registration fee is ₹600 for a non-transport light motor vehicle and ₹300 for a motorcycle. HSRP costs ₹590 for a four-wheeler and ₹320 for a two-wheeler. Registration must be completed within 30 days of purchase; the dealer normally files Form 20 and the tax online through VAHAN, and the permanent RC is issued after verification. Budget roughly one to two weeks for a direct RTO application, depending on the office and appointment slot.

How Road Tax Is Calculated in Andhra Pradesh

Andhra Pradesh uses a slab-based road tax system for both petrol and diesel vehicles. If the ex-showroom price is under ₹10 lakh, the tax is 12% of the vehicle cost. For vehicles priced above ₹10 lakh, the tax rate increases to 14%. An additional Safety Cess of 10% is levied on the tax amount. This makes AP one of the higher-tax states in India for vehicle registration.

For example, a petrol car priced at ₹8 lakh attracts 12% tax (₹96,000) plus 10% Safety Cess (₹9,600), totaling ₹1,05,600 in road tax alone. A ₹15 lakh car attracts 14% tax (₹2,10,000) plus ₹21,000 Safety Cess, totaling ₹2,31,000. Two-wheelers pay a flat 9% plus the 10% cess — a ₹1 lakh bike costs ₹9,000 in tax plus ₹900 cess, i.e. ₹9,900.

EV Exemption in Andhra Pradesh

Andhra Pradesh offers a full road tax exemption for electric vehicles (EVs) as part of its EV policy to promote green mobility. This includes battery electric cars, two-wheelers, and three-wheelers — and the registration fee (₹600 for cars, ₹300 for bikes) is also waived, so an EV pays only the HSRP charge at registration. The EV policy also supports charging infrastructure development across major cities like Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Guntur, and Tirupati.

Two-Wheeler Tax Rates

Motorcycles and scooters in Andhra Pradesh pay a flat 9% road tax regardless of price, and the 10% Safety Cess applies on the tax amount as well. The registration fee is ₹300 and HSRP costs ₹320. Two-wheeler EVs are fully exempt from both the tax and the registration fee.

Frequently asked questions

Is road tax in Andhra Pradesh one-time or annual?

Private cars, SUVs and two-wheelers pay a one-time lifetime tax at registration. Commercial vehicles are different: the 1–6 seat bracket pays a one-time ₹5,000, while larger commercial vehicles pay per seat every year.

How much is road tax for a ₹10 lakh car in Andhra Pradesh?

A ₹10 lakh petrol or diesel car falls in the 12% slab and pays ₹1,20,000 road tax plus a 10% Safety Cess of ₹12,000, or ₹1,32,000. With the ₹600 registration fee and ₹590 HSRP, the RTO total is ₹1,33,190. Above ₹10 lakh the rate rises to 14% plus the same cess.

What is the road tax for two-wheelers in Andhra Pradesh?

Two-wheelers pay a flat 9% of ex-showroom price plus the 10% Safety Cess on the tax. A ₹1,00,000 bike pays ₹9,000 tax plus ₹900 cess, or ₹9,900; registration is ₹300 and HSRP is ₹320.

Are electric vehicles exempt from road tax in Andhra Pradesh?

Yes. Electric cars, two-wheelers and three-wheelers are fully exempt from road tax for five years, and the registration fee is also waived, under the Andhra Pradesh Sustainable Electric Mobility Policy 4.0 (2024–29) issued by G.O.Ms.No. 88 dated 11 December 2024. An EV pays only HSRP — ₹590 for a car and ₹320 for a two-wheeler.

How much does HSRP cost in Andhra Pradesh?

High-Security Registration Plates cost ₹590 for four-wheelers and ₹320 for two-wheelers in Andhra Pradesh. The plate carries a unique laser-etched code and is mandatory for vehicles registered after April 2019.

Can I get a road tax refund if I sell or transfer my vehicle?

Lifetime tax is not refunded when the vehicle is sold inside Andhra Pradesh; it stays with the vehicle. If you move the vehicle to another state and re-register it there, you can apply to the original AP RTO for a refund of the unexpired portion with the NOC, re-registration proof and tax receipt. The RTO applies the refund rules and the amount is not automatic.

How do I register an out-of-state vehicle in Andhra Pradesh?

Obtain Form 27 from the original RTO, submit Form 28 for the NOC, then apply for re-registration at the local AP RTO. Road tax is recalculated on the depreciated value at the applicable AP rates, and the vehicle gets an AP registration mark after verification.

What is the penalty for late registration or late tax payment in Andhra Pradesh?

A new vehicle must be registered within 30 days of purchase. After that the RTO levies a late registration fee, and unpaid road tax attracts penalty and interest under the Andhra Pradesh Motor Vehicles Taxation Act. The exact amount depends on the delay and is calculated by the RTO.

Which RTO office should I visit in Andhra Pradesh?

Andhra Pradesh uses the AP prefix. The major offices listed on this page are Visakhapatnam Urban, Guntur, Vijayawada Urban, Kakinada, Tirupati and Nellore. Confirm the office that covers your address, and its current code, at aptransport.org before visiting.

What documents are needed to register a vehicle in Andhra Pradesh?

You need Form 20, Form 21 sale certificate, Form 22 roadworthiness certificate, valid insurance, address and identity proof, the dealer invoice, PUC certificate, HSRP order confirmation and passport-size photographs. A loan-financed vehicle also needs the hypothecation details in Form 20.
