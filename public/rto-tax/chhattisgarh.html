Vehicle Registration Process in Chhattisgarh

Chhattisgarh's vehicle registration is managed by the Chhattisgarh Transport Department with RTO offices in all districts. Major RTOs are in Raipur (CG-07), Bhilai (CG-04), Bilaspur (CG-10), Durg (CG-09), Rajnandgaon (CG-08), and Korba (CG-11). The state follows the Central Motor Vehicle Rules with state-specific taxation under the Chhattisgarh Motor Vehicles Taxation Act.

The registration process begins at the dealership where you purchase the vehicle. The dealer provides temporary registration and submits Form 20 electronically through the VAHAN portal. You then visit the RTO for physical verification of the vehicle. The RTO officer checks the engine number, chassis number, and confirms the vehicle specifications. Upon successful verification, a registration number is allotted in the CG-XX-XXXX format.

Required Documents for Registration

  • Form 20 (Vehicle Registration Application)
  • Form 21 (Dealer Sale Certificate)
  • Form 22 (Roadworthiness Certificate from Manufacturer)
  • Insurance Certificate (third-party or comprehensive)
  • Address Proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Electricity Bill, Rent Agreement)
  • Identity Proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Driving Licence)
  • Original Invoice and Delivery Note
  • Pollution Under Control Certificate
  • HSRP receipt
  • Passport-size photographs

Road Tax Calculation in Chhattisgarh

Chhattisgarh offers some of the lowest road tax rates in India. Cars pay 5% of the ex-showroom price up to ₹5 lakh and 6% above ₹5 lakh, with the same rates for petrol, diesel and CNG. Two-wheelers pay a flat 4%. This makes Chhattisgarh one of the most affordable states for vehicle registration.

For example, a ₹4 lakh car in Chhattisgarh attracts ₹20,000 in road tax (5%). A ₹10 lakh car pays 6% or ₹60,000, and a ₹20 lakh SUV pays ₹1,20,000. Compare this to neighbouring Madhya Pradesh where the same car could attract up to 10% tax for diesel models. For high-value vehicles, the savings in Chhattisgarh can be substantial, often exceeding ₹1-2 lakh compared to slab-based states.

EV Concession in Chhattisgarh

Chhattisgarh offers a 25% road tax waiver for electric vehicles — EVs pay 75% of the normal rate. For example, an EV in the 6% slab effectively pays 4.5% (₹45,000 on a ₹10 lakh car instead of ₹60,000), and an electric bike pays about 3% instead of 4%. This is a concession, not a full exemption, and the standard registration fee still applies. The state government has been promoting EV adoption in cities like Raipur and Bilaspur, with plans to set up charging stations at government buildings and public spaces.

Two-Wheeler Taxation

Two-wheelers in Chhattisgarh are taxed at a flat 4% of ex-showroom price, with a registration fee of ₹300 and HSRP of ₹200. For a commuter bike priced at ₹1 lakh, the road tax is ₹4,000 — the total registration cost is approximately ₹4,500. Electric two-wheelers get the 25% waiver, effectively paying about 3%. These low rates make Chhattisgarh an attractive state for two-wheeler buyers, encouraging personal mobility in both urban and rural areas.
