Vehicle Registration Process in Jharkhand — Step by Step

Vehicle registration in Jharkhand is managed by the Jharkhand Transport Department with RTO offices across all districts. Major RTOs include Ranchi (JH-01), Jamshedpur (JH-05), Dhanbad (JH-02), Bokaro (JH-04), Hazaribagh (JH-03), Deoghar (JH-07), and Giridih (JH-11). The state follows the Central Motor Vehicle Rules with taxation under the Jharkhand Motor Vehicles Taxation Act.

New vehicle buyers receive temporary registration from the dealer valid for 30 days. For permanent registration, you must submit Form 20 along with the required documents at the RTO. The RTO officer conducts a physical inspection of the vehicle, verifying engine and chassis numbers. Upon approval, a registration number in the JH-XX-XXXX format is issued. Jharkhand has been progressively digitising its RTO services through the VAHAN portal, making the process more efficient.

Required Documents for Registration

  • Form 20 (Vehicle Registration Application)
  • Form 21 (Certificate of Sale from Dealership)
  • Form 22 (Roadworthiness Certificate from Manufacturer)
  • Insurance Certificate (Third-party or Comprehensive)
  • Address Proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport, Utility Bill)
  • Identity Proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Driving Licence)
  • Original Vehicle Invoice and Delivery Note
  • PUC Certificate
  • HSRP booking receipt
  • Passport-size photographs (2 copies)

How Jharkhand Road Tax is Calculated

Jharkhand uses a marginal slab structure for cars: the first ₹15 lakh of the ex-showroom price is taxed at 6%, and the portion above ₹15 lakh is taxed at 15%. This is not a flat 15% on the full price — the higher rate applies only to the amount in excess of ₹15 lakh. Petrol, diesel and CNG cars all follow this same structure. Two-wheelers pay a flat 6% at any price.

Examples:

  • Car at ₹10 lakh (within ₹15 lakh limit) → 6% → ₹60,000
  • Car at ₹20 lakh → 6% on ₹15 lakh (₹90,000) + 15% on ₹5 lakh (₹75,000) → ₹1,65,000
  • Car at ₹25 lakh → 6% on ₹15 lakh (₹90,000) + 15% on ₹10 lakh (₹1,50,000) → ₹2,40,000
  • EV at ₹10 lakh → ₹60,000 minus 25% rebate → ₹45,000

The official structure for non-transport vehicles in Jharkhand (one-time tax):

  • Cars up to ₹15 lakh: 6%
  • Cars above ₹15 lakh: 6% on the first ₹15 lakh + 15% on the excess
  • Two-wheelers (all prices): 6%
  • EVs (cars and two-wheelers): 25% rebate on the computed tax

EV Concession in Jharkhand

Jharkhand does not offer a full road tax exemption for electric vehicles — instead, EVs receive a 25% rebate on the one-time road tax. The concession applies to all EV categories including electric cars and electric two-wheelers. The state has been promoting EV adoption, particularly in mining areas where air quality is a concern, and has announced plans to set up EV charging stations in Ranchi, Jamshedpur, and Dhanbad.

Two-Wheeler Taxation

Two-wheelers in Jharkhand pay a flat 6% of ex-showroom price, with a 25% rebate for electric two-wheelers, registration fee of ₹300 and HSRP of ₹200. Two-wheelers are the primary mode of personal transport in both urban and rural areas of Jharkhand, and the moderate flat rate keeps them affordable for the state's population.

Commercial Vehicle Tax in Jharkhand

Commercial vehicles in Jharkhand are taxed on a seating basis rather than by percentage of price: a one-time flat amount of ₹4,000 for vehicles up to 6 seats, and annual per-seat tax of ₹1,500 per seat for 7-12 seats and ₹2,500 per seat beyond 12 seats.
