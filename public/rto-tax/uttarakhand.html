Vehicle Registration Process in Uttarakhand

Uttarakhand, the "Land of the Gods," operates its vehicle registration system through the Uttarakhand Transport Department with RTO offices in Dehradun (UK 07), Haridwar (UK 08), Rishikesh (UK 14), Nainital (UK 04), Haldwani (UK 05), Roorkee (UK 16), Rudrapur (UK 06), Kashipur (UK 17), Almora (UK 01), Pithoragarh (UK 02), and Uttarkashi (UK 11). The Dehradun RTO handles the highest registration volume, followed by Haridwar and Haldwani. Uttarakhand was formed in 2000 from the hill districts of Uttar Pradesh, and its transport department has since developed its own distinct registration policies tailored to the state's unique geography.

The state's terrain ranges from the fertile Doon valley and Terai plains to the high Himalayan peaks, creating diverse vehicle requirements across its regions. In the plains districts of Dehradun, Haridwar, and Udham Singh Nagar, standard passenger cars and two-wheelers dominate. In the hill districts of Pauri, Almora, Pithoragarh, and Chamoli, SUVs and 4x4 vehicles are preferred due to steep gradients and narrow mountain roads. Uttarakhand also sees a significant number of tourist vehicle registrations, particularly for the Char Dham Yatra (Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath, Badrinath) routes and popular hill stations like Nainital, Mussoorie, and Ranikhet. The state has been implementing a comprehensive road safety program, including mandatory HSRP enforcement and strict PUC compliance checks at entry points to hill areas.

  1. Buy the vehicle and get a temporary registration. The dealer issues a temporary registration (application in Form 20) at the time of purchase. A temporary registration is valid for a maximum of one month (Rule 47, Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989), within which the permanent registration must be completed.
  2. Submit the registration application. Form 20 (application for registration), Form 21 (sale certificate from the dealer/manufacturer), Form 22 (certificate of roadworthiness), valid insurance, and proof of address and identity are filed — normally by the dealer for new vehicles.
  3. Pay road tax and fees. Road tax is a one-time payment at registration. The registration fee under Rule 81 of CMVR 1989 is ₹300 for a motorcycle and ₹600 for a non-transport light motor vehicle.
  4. Registration number is allotted. On verification of documents and tax payment, VAHAN allots a permanent number in the UK-XX-XXXX format and the RC is issued to the owner.
  5. Get the HSRP fitted. New vehicles get the High Security Registration Plate fitted at the dealer point or RTO-approved vendor before delivery.

How Road Tax Is Calculated in Uttarakhand

Uttarakhand offers some of the lowest road tax rates in North India, with a two-slab structure designed to keep vehicle ownership affordable in the hill state. All fuels are treated the same — there is no diesel surcharge. Cars priced up to ₹10 lakh (ex-showroom) pay 6%, and cars above ₹10 lakh pay 8%, as a one-time road tax. Electric vehicles are fully exempt from road tax per current policy.

Examples:

  • A petrol car at ₹8,00,000 falls in the up-to-₹10 lakh slab: road tax = ₹8,00,000 × 6% = ₹48,000, plus ₹600 + ₹400 = ₹49,000 total.
  • A car at ₹12,00,000 falls in the above-₹10 lakh slab: road tax = ₹12,00,000 × 8% = ₹96,000, plus ₹600 + ₹400 = ₹97,000 total.
  • An EV at ₹12,00,000 pays 0% road tax — only ₹1,000 in registration fee and HSRP.
  • A two-wheeler at ₹80,000 pays 6% = ₹4,800, plus ₹300 registration fee and HSRP.

Electric Vehicle (EV) Exemption Policy

Uttarakhand provides a full (100%) road tax exemption for electric vehicles under current state policy, supporting the national goal of 30% EV penetration by 2030. The exemption applies to all EV categories without a price cap — for both cars and two-wheelers — although the latest extension of the exemption notification is unverified. Given Uttarakhand's fragile Himalayan ecosystem, the state has a strong interest in promoting zero-emission vehicles, and Dehradun and Haridwar have already seen the installation of public EV charging stations under this initiative. Registration fee (₹600 for cars) and HSRP (₹400) still apply. Always confirm the current notification with the Uttarakhand Transport Department before finalising a purchase.

Where to Pay Uttarakhand Road Tax Online

Road tax can be paid online on VAHAN (vahan.parivahan.gov.in): enter the vehicle registration number → Online Services → Pay Vehicle Tax → validate with the last 5 digits of the chassis number → enter the OTP sent to your registered mobile number → update insurance details → review the fee panel → pay. For new vehicles, the dealer point or RTO collects the tax during registration itself.

Required Documents for Vehicle Registration

  • Form 20 (duly filled application form)
  • Form 21 (Sale Certificate from authorised dealer)
  • Form 22 (Certificate of Roadworthiness)
  • Insurance Certificate (Third-party or Comprehensive)
  • Address Proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport, Utility Bill)
  • Identity Proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Driving Licence)
  • Original Invoice and Delivery Challan
  • PUC Certificate
  • Passport-size photographs (2 copies)

Residents of hill districts may also need to provide additional proof of residence if applying at an RTO outside their home district.
