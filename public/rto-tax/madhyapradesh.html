Vehicle Registration Process in Madhya Pradesh — Step by Step

Madhya Pradesh vehicle registration is managed by the MP Transport Department with RTO offices across all districts. Major RTOs include Bhopal (MP-04), Indore (MP-09), Jabalpur (MP-20), Gwalior (MP-07), Ujjain (MP-13), Sagar (MP-15), Rewa (MP-17), Guna (MP-08), Chhindwara (MP-17), and Ratlam (MP-12). The state follows the Central Motor Vehicle Rules with taxation under the MP Motor Vehicles Taxation Act.

One unique aspect of Madhya Pradesh is its quarterly tax payment model. Unlike most states where road tax is paid as a one-time lump sum at registration, MP operates on a quarterly payment basis. This can be advantageous for buyers who prefer lower upfront costs, though the total payment over the vehicle's life may be higher due to periodic collection charges. The registration process otherwise follows the standard VAHAN framework with dealer-assisted temporary registration followed by permanent registration at the RTO.

Required Documents for Registration

  • Form 20 (Vehicle Registration Application)
  • Form 21 (Sale Certificate from Dealer)
  • Form 22 (Roadworthiness Certificate from Manufacturer)
  • Valid Insurance Certificate (Third-party or Comprehensive)
  • Address Proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport, Electricity Bill)
  • Identity Proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Driving Licence)
  • Original Vehicle Invoice and Delivery Note
  • PUC Certificate
  • HSRP booking receipt
  • Passport-size photographs (2 copies)

How Madhya Pradesh Road Tax is Calculated

Madhya Pradesh uses a slab-based road tax system with different rates for petrol and diesel vehicles. Petrol and CNG vehicles are taxed at 8% for vehicles up to ₹10 lakh, 10% for ₹10-20 lakh, and 14% for above ₹20 lakh. Diesel vehicles face higher rates of 10%, 12%, and 16% respectively across the same slabs.

Examples:

  • ₹8 lakh petrol car → 8% → ₹64,000
  • ₹12 lakh diesel SUV → 12% → ₹1,44,000
  • ₹30 lakh diesel car → 16% → ₹4,80,000
  • ₹10 lakh EV → 4% → ₹40,000

The official slab structure for non-transport vehicles in Madhya Pradesh:

  • Petrol/CNG cars up to ₹10 lakh: 8%
  • Petrol/CNG cars ₹10-20 lakh: 10%
  • Petrol/CNG cars above ₹20 lakh: 14%
  • Diesel cars up to ₹10 lakh: 10%
  • Diesel cars ₹10-20 lakh: 12%
  • Diesel cars above ₹20 lakh: 16%
  • Two-wheelers (all prices): 8%
  • EVs (cars and two-wheelers): 4%

All private-vehicle tax in MP is levied on a quarterly payment model.

EV Policy in Madhya Pradesh

Madhya Pradesh's full EV exemption expired on 26 March 2026. EVs are now charged a flat 4% road tax — for a ₹10 lakh EV, that is ₹40,000 — which is lower than the 8-14% charged on petrol cars but no longer zero. Electric two-wheelers also pay 4% against the 8% for petrol bikes. Buyers should confirm the current notification with the MP Transport Department before finalising an EV purchase.

Quarterly Tax Model

MP's quarterly tax system means vehicle owners pay road tax on a quarterly basis rather than a one-time lump sum at registration. While this reduces the upfront cost at registration, the total tax over 15 years can be higher due to administrative charges.

Commercial Vehicle Tax in Madhya Pradesh

Commercial vehicles in MP are taxed on a seating basis rather than by percentage of price: a one-time flat amount of ₹4,000 for vehicles up to 6 seats, and annual per-seat tax of ₹1,200 per seat for 7-12 seats, ₹2,000 per seat for 13-32 seats and ₹3,000 per seat beyond 32 seats.
