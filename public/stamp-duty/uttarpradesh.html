Stamp duty rates in Uttar Pradesh

Uttar Pradesh uses a statewide stamp duty schedule, so the headline percentage does not change when you cross from Noida to Ghaziabad or from Lucknow to Kanpur. What changes is the circle rate fixed for the locality. The rate table above is the verified schedule used by the DesiCalc calculator.

By buyer gender

A male buyer pays 7% stamp duty. A female buyer pays 6% on properties valued up to ₹1 crore, and the same 6% applies when all co-owners are women. Above ₹1 crore the female and joint female rate rises to 7%. A mixed male and female joint purchase pays 6.5% at every value. Two male co-owners pay 7%, the same as a sole male buyer.

The female concession is capped by value rather than by buyer age or first-home status. On a ₹60,00,000 property a woman pays ₹3,60,000 at 6%, compared with ₹4,20,000 for a man at 7%. On a ₹1,20,00,000 property the female rate is 7%, so the concession no longer applies. The threshold applies to the property value used for duty, not to the buyer's income.

By property location

Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Lucknow, Kanpur, Varanasi, Agra and every other district use the same state percentages. A ₹60,00,000 property in Noida and a ₹60,00,000 property in Lucknow therefore attract the same stamp duty if the taxable value is the same. The difference comes from the circle rate: Noida and Greater Noida have some of the highest circle rates in the state, so a similarly sized flat can have a much higher taxable value there.

Within the NCR belt, the development authority and the district administration publish locality-wise circle rates. A sector in Noida can have a different rate from a neighbouring sector, and Ghaziabad can differ from both. Buyers should check the current circle rate for the exact sector, locality and property type rather than relying on a city-wide average.

Registration charges

Registration in Uttar Pradesh is 1% of the property value. The calculator applies no upper cap, so a ₹60,00,000 property has a registration fee of ₹60,000. The registering office may apply a small minimum fee where the calculated amount is very low; that does not affect normal residential transactions.

The calculator adds no separate stamp duty cess beyond the percentages in the table. Development authority charges, lease rent, transfer fees and annual maintenance are separate from stamp duty and registration. In Noida and Greater Noida, buyers should budget for the authority's own transfer and lease charges in addition to the government stamp duty.

How stamp duty is calculated in Uttar Pradesh

The formula is stamp duty = higher of circle rate and sale consideration × applicable rate. Registration is 1% of the same value. The applicable rate depends on the buyer category and, for female and joint female buyers, on whether the value is above ₹1 crore.

Circle rate vs market rate

Uttar Pradesh circle rates are set by the district collector and vary by locality, property type and land use. The stamp duty department uses the higher of the circle rate and the actual sale price. If the agreed price is below the circle rate, the circle rate is used. If the market price is higher, the market price is used. The current rates are published through the IGRSUP portal and the district stamp offices.

This rule can surprise buyers in fast-appreciating Noida and Ghaziabad sectors, where the circle rate may be revised upward after the deal is negotiated. It also matters for resale flats and plots where the declared consideration may be lower than the authority's valuation. Obtain the current circle rate before signing and keep the valuation basis in the deed file.

On a property above ₹1 crore the female rate becomes 7%, so the female total matches the male total at the same value.

Other costs to budget for

Stamp duty and registration are only part of the cash outlay. Legal fees for title verification and deed drafting, notary charges, e-stamp facilitation and documentation costs are separate. In Noida and Greater Noida, the development authority may charge transfer fees, lease rent and other charges for leasehold properties. For a resale flat, check maintenance dues, property tax arrears and society transfer charges before closing.

Documents required for registration in Uttar Pradesh

Registration is handled by the Inspector General of Registration through the Department of Stamps and Registration. The Sub-Registrar's checklist can vary by property, but the following documents are commonly required:

  • Sale deed prepared on e-stamp paper of the correct value
  • Previous title deed and the chain of ownership documents
  • Encumbrance certificate for the relevant period
  • Khatauni or record of rights from the revenue department
  • Property tax receipts and dues clearance certificate
  • Approved map or building plan for constructed property
  • Aadhaar card, PAN card and voter ID or passport of all parties
  • Passport-size photographs of buyer, seller and witnesses
  • NOC from the development authority for Noida or Greater Noida where applicable

For agricultural land, expect additional revenue records and land-use documents. For inherited property, keep the will, succession certificate or family settlement. For a development authority flat, keep the allotment letter, lease deed and possession letter. Ask the Sub-Registrar office for its current checklist before the appointment.

Registration process in Uttar Pradesh

Step-by-step registration

  1. Verify title and circle rate. Check the chain of title, encumbrance and the current circle rate for the specific locality.
  2. Pay stamp duty online. Use the IGRSUP e-stamping facility or an authorised bank, and keep the e-stamp certificate.
  3. Draft the sale deed. Include the property description, consideration, ownership shares and the stamp duty details.
  4. Book the Sub-Registrar appointment. Use the IGRSUP portal to choose the office with jurisdiction over the property and a convenient slot.
  5. Upload documents and attend. Upload the deed and supporting documents, then appear with the originals for biometric verification and photographs.
  6. Pay registration and complete. Pay the 1% registration fee and collect the receipt and registered deed.
  7. Complete mutation. Follow up with the revenue office to update the record of rights in the buyer's name.

Timeline and practical tips

The registration appointment is usually completed on the booked day if the e-stamp and documents are ready. The old page noted a typical processing window of 7 to 14 working days from presentation, but the exact time depends on the office and the completeness of the file. Mutation is a separate step and can take longer. Keep certified copies of the registered deed, payment receipts and the encumbrance search.

Ways to reduce stamp duty legally in Uttar Pradesh

Female and joint ownership

The main legal route is the female concession. A sole female buyer or a joint purchase by women pays 6% instead of 7% up to ₹1 crore. On a ₹60,00,000 property that saves ₹60,000 in stamp duty. A mixed male and female joint purchase pays 6.5%, which saves ₹30,000 compared with the male rate. The ownership should reflect the actual contribution and the intended succession, not just the tax rate.

Correct valuation and notified exemptions

Use the correct circle rate and declare the real consideration. Under-declaration does not reduce the lawful duty because the district office applies the circle rate, and it can create problems at mutation or resale. If a transfer falls within a family settlement, gift or affordable-housing notification, confirm the exact eligibility and documents with the Sub-Registrar or a qualified advisor. The calculator applies only the published gender and joint-ownership rates.
