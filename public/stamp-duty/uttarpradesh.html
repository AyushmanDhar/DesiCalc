Overview of Real Estate in Uttar Pradesh

Uttar Pradesh, India's most populous state, has one of the most diverse and dynamic real estate markets in the country. The state encompasses the National Capital Region (NCR) cities of Noida and Greater Noida, which are among India's most active property markets, along with the state capital Lucknow, the spiritual capital Varanasi, the industrial city Kanpur, and the tourism hub Agra. The Noida and Greater Noida regions, governed by the Noida Authority and YEIDA, have seen massive real estate development with world-class infrastructure, expressways, and the upcoming Jewar International Airport. The Uttar Pradesh government has been proactive in real estate reforms, including the UP RERA authority and single-window clearance systems for project approvals.

Lucknow, the state capital, has emerged as a strong residential market with localities like Gomti Nagar, Hazratganj, and Aliganj commanding premium prices. Varanasi and Ayodhya have seen an infrastructure and tourism-driven uptick in property demand. The state's extensive network of expressways — including the Purvanchal, Bundelkhand, and Ganga expressways — continues to open up new growth corridors for both residential and industrial real estate.

How Stamp Duty is Calculated in Uttar Pradesh

Stamp duty in Uttar Pradesh is calculated on the higher of the circle rate or the actual sale price. Circle rates in UP are set by the District Magistrate and vary by locality — Noida sectors have among the highest circle rates in the state. Check current rates on the IGRS UP portal (igrsup.gov.in) under the "Stamp and Registration" section.

Male buyers pay 7% stamp duty; female buyers pay 6% — a 1% concession that applies to properties valued up to ₹1 crore. Joint ownership with all female co-owners is also 6%. Mixed-gender joint ownership is 6.5%, and joint ownership with all male co-owners is 7%. The registration charge is a flat 1% across all cities, with no upper cap.

Quick example: On a ₹50 lakh property in Lucknow, a male buyer pays ₹3.5 lakh stamp duty (7%) + ₹50,000 registration (1%) = ₹4 lakh total (8%). A female buyer pays ₹3 lakh + ₹50,000 = ₹3.5 lakh total (7%), saving ₹50,000.

Property Registration Process in Uttar Pradesh

Property registration in Uttar Pradesh is managed by the Inspector General of Registration through the Department of Stamps and Registration, with the online IGRS UP portal (igrsup.gov.in). Here are the key steps:

  • Step 1: Draft the sale deed with a licensed deed writer or advocate and get it vetted.
  • Step 2: Pay stamp duty through e-stamping on the IGRS UP portal or at authorised banks. The e-stamp certificate serves as proof of payment.
  • Step 3: Apply for registration on the portal, upload the required documents, and book an appointment slot at the Sub-Registrar office.
  • Step 4: Both buyer and seller appear before the Sub-Registrar with original documents and two witnesses. Biometric verification and photography are completed.
  • Step 5: The Sub-Registrar verifies the deed and payments. Upon approval, the deed is registered and the registered copy can be downloaded from the portal.

Documents Required

  • Sale deed on e-stamp paper of appropriate value
  • Previous title deed and chain of ownership documents
  • Encumbrance certificate for the last 13 years
  • Khatauni or record of rights from the revenue department
  • Property tax receipts and dues clearance certificate
  • Approved map or building plan (for constructed property)
  • Aadhaar card, PAN card, and Voter ID of all parties
  • Passport-size photographs
  • NOC from the development authority (for Noida/Greater Noida)
