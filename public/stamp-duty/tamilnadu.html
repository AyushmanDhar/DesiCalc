How Stamp Duty is Calculated in Tamil Nadu

Stamp duty in Tamil Nadu is calculated on the higher of the transaction value or the guideline value (ready reckoner rate) fixed by the Registration department. The state charges a flat stamp duty of 7% for all buyers — male, female, or joint — with no stamp duty concession for women or any other category.

Registration charges are 4% of the property value. Since 1 April 2025, female buyers registering properties valued up to ₹10 lakh pay a reduced registration charge of 3%. For properties above ₹10 lakh, the standard 4% applies to all buyers. The total cost is therefore 11% for most transactions and 10% for women buying properties up to ₹10 lakh.

Property Registration Process in Tamil Nadu

The registration process in Tamil Nadu is managed by the Registration department through the TN REGINET portal. Here are the key steps:

  • Step 1: Prepare the sale deed with the assistance of a document writer or lawyer. The draft is reviewed by the Sub-Registrar office.
  • Step 2: Pay stamp duty through e-stamping or via the online portal. The e-stamp certificate is required at the time of registration.
  • Step 3: Book an appointment at the Sub-Registrar office (SRO) having jurisdiction over the property through the TN REGINET portal.
  • Step 4: Both parties appear before the Sub-Registrar with original documents and witnesses. Biometric verification and photography are completed.
  • Step 5: The Sub-Registrar verifies the deed, guideline value compliance, and payments. Upon satisfaction, the deed is registered and a copy is provided.

Documents Required

  • Sale deed on e-stamp paper
  • Previous title documents (parent deed and chain of ownership)
  • Encumbrance certificate
  • Patta and chitta (land records)
  • Property tax receipts
  • Aadhaar card and PAN card
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Identity proof of witnesses
