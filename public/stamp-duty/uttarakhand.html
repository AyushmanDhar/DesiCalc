Overview of Real Estate in Uttarakhand

Uttarakhand's real estate market centres on Dehradun, the state capital and largest city, along with Haridwar, Rishikesh, Nainital, Haldwani and Rudrapur. Dehradun offers the strongest residential growth, with localities such as Rajpur Road, Sahastradhara Road, Vasant Vihar and the Doon University belt attracting end-users and NRIs. Rudrapur and Kashipur, with the SIDCUL industrial estate, drive commercial demand. The hill towns of Nainital, Mussoorie, Almora and Ranikhet continue to attract second-home and holiday-property buyers.

Uttarakhand's proximity to Delhi and the planned Delhi–Dehradun expressway has made towns along the corridor — including Roorkee and Haridwar — increasingly popular with buyers from the NCR. The state has an active RERA regime, and circle rates (collector rates) are notified by the state government and revised periodically. They form the minimum valuation on which stamp duty is computed.

Uttarakhand's stamp duty structure is among the most buyer-friendly in north India: a flat 5% for men, a 25% concession (3.75%) for women on properties worth up to ₹25 lakh, and a registration fee of 2% capped at ₹50,000. Above ₹25 lakh, the full 5% rate applies to the entire property value.

How Stamp Duty is Calculated in Uttarakhand

Stamp duty in Uttarakhand is calculated on the higher of the agreement value or the circle rate notified for the locality. Male buyers pay a flat 5% at all property values. Female buyers pay 3.75% when the property is worth up to ₹25 lakh and the full 5% above that. Mixed-gender joint ownership is charged at 4.37% up to ₹25 lakh and 5% above. The concession is a whole-value benefit — it applies only when the total property value is ₹25 lakh or less. Registration is 2% of the property value, capped at ₹50,000.

For example, a female buyer of a ₹30 lakh property (above the concession limit) pays the full 5% — ₹1,50,000 in stamp duty. The registration fee of 2% on ₹30 lakh (₹60,000) is capped at ₹50,000, making the total ₹2,00,000. A male buyer of the same property pays the same ₹2,00,000.

Property Registration Process in Uttarakhand

The registration process in Uttarakhand is managed through the state's Sub-Registrar offices. Here are the steps:

  • Step 1: Prepare the sale deed and get it drafted by a registered deed writer or advocate.
  • Step 2: Pay stamp duty through e-stamping via SHCIL (shcilestamp.com) or the state's official registration portal (registration.uk.gov.in).
  • Step 3: Book an appointment at the Sub-Registrar office for the concerned tehsil, either online through the registration portal or in person at the office.
  • Step 4: Appear at the Sub-Registrar office with all original documents. Both parties must be present with two witnesses. Biometric verification and photography are conducted.
  • Step 5: The deed is registered after verification and payment of the 2% registration fee (capped at ₹50,000). The registered document is returned along with the registration receipt.
