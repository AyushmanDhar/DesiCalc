Overview of Real Estate in Manipur

Manipur, one of the seven sisters of Northeast India, has a steadily growing real estate market centred on its capital Imphal and emerging urban centres like Bishnupur and Thoubal. Property prices in Manipur are relatively moderate compared to the national average, making it an accessible market for homebuyers. The Imphal International Airport expansion and improved road connectivity under the Bharatmala project have positively influenced property values in and around the capital.

A defining feature of Manipur's property market is its geography: land can generally be purchased only in the valley areas (roughly 10% of the state), while the five hill districts — Churachandpur, Ukhrul, Senapati, Tamenglong, and Chandel — have strict protections on land transfer to non-tribals under the Manipur (Hill Areas) District Councils. An Inner Line Permit regime further restricts non-resident purchase. Within the valley, Imphal East and Imphal West command the highest values, driven by government employment, commerce, and urban infrastructure.

How Stamp Duty is Calculated in Manipur

Stamp duty in Manipur is a flat 7% applied to the higher of the agreement (transaction) value or the government-fixed circle rate (guidance value) for the locality. The same 7% rate applies to every buyer category — male, female, and joint ownership — with no gender concession. There is no slab structure: the rate does not change with property value.

Registration charges are 3% of the property value, capped at a maximum of ₹2,50,000. The cap means that once the property value exceeds roughly ₹83.3 lakh, the registration fee stops growing and stays fixed at ₹2,50,000. Manipur levies no additional surcharge, cess, or local body tax — the total cost is simply stamp duty plus the registration charge.

For a property valued at ₹50 lakh in Imphal (higher of agreement value or circle rate):

Component Rate Amount (₹50L property)
Stamp duty 7% ₹3,50,000
Registration charges 3% (below ₹2,50,000 cap) ₹1,50,000
Total cost 10% ₹5,00,000

Note: The same ₹5,00,000 total applies to male, female, and joint buyers — Manipur offers no gender concession. Verify current rates with the Sub-Registrar office before payment.

Property Registration Process in Manipur

Property registration in Manipur is administered under the Indian Stamp Act, 1899, and the Registration Act, 1908, through the Department of Revenue, Government of Manipur. The state uses the National Generic Document Registration System (NGDRS) portal for online registration. Here are the key steps:

  • Step 1: Pay the stamp duty and generate the e-stamp (available through SHCIL for amounts above ₹500) or purchase the stamp paper from an authorised seller.
  • Step 2: Register on the NGDRS Manipur portal, log in, and fill out the registration form with property and party details.
  • Step 3: Book an appointment at the Sub-Registrar office through the portal.
  • Step 4: Buyer, seller, and two witnesses appear at the Sub-Registrar office with original documents and identity proof.
  • Step 5: The Sub-Registrar verifies the deed and payments; upon satisfaction, the deed is registered and a certified copy is provided.

Documents Required

  • Sale deed (executed on e-stamp or stamp paper)
  • Previous title documents (chain of ownership)
  • Encumbrance certificate
  • Land record documents (survey number, plot details)
  • Property tax receipts
  • Aadhaar card and PAN card of buyer and seller
  • Passport-size photographs of parties and witnesses
  • Identity and address proof of buyer, seller, and witnesses
  • Valuation certificate and power of attorney (if applicable)
