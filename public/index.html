How DesiCalc works

  1. Pick the calculator that matches your question — income tax, stamp duty, RTO road tax, HRA or GST.
  2. Enter your numbers. The forms accept annual income, property value, vehicle price, salary structure or invoice amount, and you can share the result through a link.
  3. Get an instant breakdown that shows every slab, rate and fee used, so you can check the result instead of trusting a single total.

The work happens on your device. There is no submission step, no queue and no email required, which means the numbers update as you type and the page still works after your first visit if you go offline.

What you can calculate

Income tax

The income tax calculator compares the old and new regimes for FY 2026-27. The new regime starts at nil up to ₹4 lakh, then applies 5% to ₹8 lakh, 10% to ₹12 lakh, 15% to ₹16 lakh, 20% to ₹20 lakh, 25% to ₹24 lakh and 30% above that. The old regime uses the familiar ₹2.5 lakh exemption, raised to ₹3 lakh for senior citizens and ₹5 lakh for super senior citizens, with 5%, 20% and 30% bands above it. Both paths apply the standard deduction, the Section 87A rebate, surcharge where income crosses the thresholds, and the 4% health and education cess.

Stamp duty

The stamp duty calculator covers 28 states plus Delhi. Each state page carries its own rates, including the concessions some states give to women buyers or joint ownership, and the calculator adds the registration fee, metro cess and surcharge where the state levies them. You can see the stamp duty and the registration charge separately instead of one bundled figure.

RTO road tax

The RTO road tax calculator covers 28 states plus Delhi for private cars, two-wheelers and commercial vehicles. Some states charge a percentage of the vehicle price in slabs, some charge a flat percentage, and some use per-CC rates. Electric vehicles follow the exemption or concession notified by each state, and the result includes the registration fee and HSRP cost alongside the road tax.

HRA exemption

The HRA calculator applies the Section 10(13A) rule to salaried employees: the exempt amount is the least of the HRA actually received, rent paid minus 10% of basic salary plus dearness allowance, and 50% of basic salary plus dearness allowance in metro cities or 40% elsewhere. Enter your salary structure and rent to see which of the three limits applies and how much of your HRA becomes taxable.

GST

GST regular rates are 0%, 5%, 18% and 40%, with 3% on gold and silver and 0.25% on rough diamonds. The composition scheme uses flat rates of 1% for traders, 5% for restaurants and 6% for service providers. You can add GST to a base amount or extract it from an inclusive price, and the breakdown shows the CGST, SGST or IGST split so the invoice total is easy to check.

Why rates change every year

Income tax slabs, rebates and surcharge bands can move with the Union Budget. Stamp duty and RTO road tax are state subjects, so a state notification can change a rate, a registration fee or a concession between budgets. Electric vehicle incentives are revised often and differ from one state to the next. DesiCalc checks each stored rate against the primary source before publishing it, records the review date on every page, and publishes a correction when a source changes. You can read the full process on our methodology page.

About DesiCalc

DesiCalc is built for people who want to check a number quickly and move on. There is no account, no email wall and no advertising script on the site. The calculators store nothing on a server: your inputs stay in the browser and are cleared when you close the tab. Rates are checked against gazette notifications, income tax and transport department pages, and the official portals listed on our methodology page, and we publish a correction when a source changes.
