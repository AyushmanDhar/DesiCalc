About DesiCalc

DesiCalc provides free, accurate financial calculators for Indian taxpayers and property buyers. No registration, no data upload — everything runs in your browser.

What We Offer

  • Stamp Duty Calculator — Calculate property registration charges for all 28 states including female concession, circle rate, and first-time buyer benefits.
  • RTO Road Tax Calculator — Compute vehicle registration tax for all states with accurate state-wise rates.
  • Income Tax Calculator — Estimate your tax under old and new regime for FY 2026-27.
  • Guides & Comparisons — Detailed guides on ITR filing, stamp duty, RTO registration, and tax regime comparisons.

Data Sources

All tax rates are sourced from respective state government publications, transport department notifications, and the Income Tax Department of India. Income-tax rates are updated for FY 2026-27. State stamp duty and RTO tax rates were re-verified in October 2026 against official government sources.

How We Verify Our Rates

Every stamp duty and RTO tax rate is checked against official state government publications, transport department notifications, and the Income Tax Department of India. Each state page links to its Sources & References. Rates are re-verified whenever a state government issues a new notification.

Editorial Standards

DesiCalc is run independently and stays intentionally anonymous. We do not provide financial advice and we never invent credentials. If you spot an error, contact us at desicalc@gmail.com and we will correct it and update the page promptly.
