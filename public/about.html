About DesiCalc

Published by the DesiCalc Editorial Team. Last reviewed 2026-10-30.

DesiCalc is a free collection of Indian tax and financial calculators for people who need a quick, trustworthy estimate without handing over personal data or signing up for an account. Stamp duty, RTO road tax, income tax, HRA exemption, and GST are all covered in one place, in both English and Hinglish.

What DesiCalc is

DesiCalc is a set of calculators and short guides covering the charges that come up when you buy property, register a vehicle, or file a return. The stamp duty calculator handles all 28 states plus Delhi, including female and joint-ownership concessions where the state offers them. The RTO calculator estimates road tax and registration charges for private and commercial vehicles. The income tax calculator compares the old and new regimes for FY 2026-27. HRA and GST tools sit alongside them, and longer guides explain the rules behind each one.

Why we built it

Most people meet these numbers at the worst possible moment: at the registrar's counter, at the dealership, or a week before the filing deadline. The official portals are built for officials, not for someone trying to work out whether they can afford a flat. We wanted a page that loads instantly, works on a phone, and tells you the number without asking for your name, PAN, or email first.

How it works

Every calculation runs in your browser. The rate tables and slab data are downloaded as plain JavaScript files, and the arithmetic happens on your device. Nothing you type is sent to a server, because there is no server-side calculator to send it to. Your inputs are not uploaded, not stored in an account, and not shared with anyone. The optional convenience features, such as restoring your last inputs or saving a calculation link, use your own browser storage or the URL itself, so the data stays with you.

How we keep rates accurate

Rates change with budgets and state notifications, so accuracy is an ongoing job rather than a one-time check. Each figure is tied to an official source, compared against the value already in our data files, and changed only when the source itself changed. The full process, including the source hierarchy we follow and the changelog of what has moved, is published on our methodology page. If something looks wrong, that page also explains how to report it.

How we are funded

DesiCalc runs no ads and has no paid plans. There are no sponsored results inside the calculators, no affiliate links in the guides, and no upsell to a paid report. Hosting is provided on Cloudflare Pages, which keeps the running cost low enough that the site can stay free. If that model ever changes, the change will be disclosed here and on our editorial policy before anything commercial appears on a page.

Contact

Feedback, source requests, and corrections are welcome. Use the contact page for general messages, or email desicalc@gmail.com if you have a notification or official page that shows a rate is out of date. Verified errors are corrected within 48 hours.
